# StreakSync — Puzzle Tracker

Share your Wordle, Connections, Queens or Pips result and StreakSync logs it
automatically. 15 daily puzzles, one dashboard, every streak safe.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/streaksync-puzzle-tracker/id6755203446)**

---

## Supported games

**Wordle & variants** — Wordle, Quordle, Octordle, Nerdle
**NYT** — Connections, Spelling Bee, Mini Crossword, Strands, Pips
**LinkedIn** — Queens, Tango, Crossclimb, Pinpoint, Zip, Mini Sudoku

## How it works

Finish a puzzle, tap Share inside the game, and pick StreakSync. The result is
parsed automatically and your streak updates — no manual entry.
